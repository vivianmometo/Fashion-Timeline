Did you know?

1800s

The 1800s was characterized by modest clothing with an emphasis on formality. Corsets and full skirts were common in women's fashion, while men wore tailcoats and trousers.

1900s

The early 1900s saw the introduction of more relaxed styles, with the flapper dress becoming a symbol of the 1920s. The men's suit also evolved to reflect the changing tastes of the time.

1920s

The 1920s, also known as the Roaring Twenties, was a time of bold fashion. Women's dresses were shorter, and bobbed hair became popular. Men's fashion became more relaxed with a focus on comfort and elegance.

1960s

The 1960s brought a revolution in fashion with the rise of the youth culture. Miniskirts, bell-bottoms, and psychedelic patterns became iconic, while men embraced more casual attire with slim-fit suits.

1980s

The 1980s were known for bold fashion statements, with oversized clothing, neon colors, and big hairstyles. Popular trends included power suits for women and casual streetwear for men.

2000s

The 2000s saw a mix of styles, from low-rise jeans and crop tops to vintage-inspired looks. Technology influenced fashion with the rise of social media, bringing street style into the mainstream.
