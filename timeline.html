Key Designers

Charles Frederick Worth

Considered the first fashion designer, Worth established the House of Worth in Paris in the mid-1800s. He is known for his lavish haute couture gowns.

Jeanne Paquin

One of the first female couturiers, Jeanne Paquin was renowned for her elegant designs and was the first woman to open a fashion house in Paris.

Coco Chanel

Coco Chanel revolutionized women's fashion by introducing more comfortable yet stylish clothing, including the iconic little black dress.

Mary Quant

Mary Quant was a British fashion designer credited with popularizing the miniskirt in the 1960s, an iconic symbol of the youth culture movement.

Vivienne Westwood

Known for her role in the punk movement, Vivienne Westwood's designs challenge conventional fashion norms, mixing modern styles with historical references.

Alexander McQueen

Known for his dramatic and bold designs, McQueen’s collections often combine dark, romantic, and avant-garde elements to challenge traditional fashion aesthetics.
